ToneScope Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2025

ToneScope ("we", "our", or "us") is a music tuner, metronome, and recorder app. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Data We Collect

  • Microphone audio: Used in real-time for pitch detection and recording. Audio is processed locally on your device. We do not transmit, store, or upload any audio.
  • App preferences: Settings such as theme, language, BPM, and recording format are saved locally on your device using browser localStorage. This data never leaves your device.
  • Purchase information: On paid (Pro) versions, purchase status is verified through RevenueCat. RevenueCat may collect a random user identifier and purchase receipt data. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • Advertising (free version only): The free version displays ads via Google AdMob (native) or Google AdSense (web). Google may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads. On iOS, you will be asked for permission before any tracking occurs. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Feedback: If you submit feedback through the app, your message is sent to our email via FormSubmit. No other data is collected.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not create user accounts or collect names, email addresses, or contact information.
  • We do not sell your data to third parties.
  • We do not collect location data.
  • Audio recordings stay on your device and are deleted when you delete them in the app.

3. Third-Party Services

  • RevenueCat — in-app purchase management. Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob / AdSense — advertising (free version). Privacy Policy
  • FormSubmit — feedback delivery. Privacy Policy

4. App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS 14.5 and later, ToneScope asks for permission before allowing advertising partners to track your activity across apps and websites. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy → Tracking.

5. Children

ToneScope is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
