Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the ToneScope mobile and web application ("ToneScope", "the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. By using ToneScope you agree to the practices described below.

Summary: ToneScope processes audio entirely on your device. We do not collect personal information, do not create user accounts, and do not upload your recordings or microphone data to any server.

1. Information We Process

  • Microphone audio. The tuner, spectrum analyzer, and recording features access your device's microphone. Audio is analyzed and stored on your device only and is never transmitted to us or any third party.
  • Local app data. Your recordings, preferences (theme, language, reference pitch, tuner sensitivity, metronome settings, etc.), and purchase state are stored locally on your device using the platform's standard storage.
  • Purchase information. If you purchase ToneScope Pro, the transaction is processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. A purchase receipt is passed to RevenueCat to validate and restore your purchase across devices. See "Third-Party Services" below.
  • Advertising data (free version only). The free version may display banner ads provided by a mobile ad network. The ad network may collect limited device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier) to serve relevant ads. You can limit or reset this identifier in your device settings.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require you to create an account.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.
  • We do not upload, store, or transmit your audio recordings off your device.
  • We do not sell your information to third parties.

3. Permissions We Request

  • Microphone — required for pitch detection, spectrum visualization, and audio recording. Audio never leaves your device.
  • Storage / Files — used only when you explicitly export or share a recording.
  • Network — used to load ads (free version) and validate in-app purchases.

4. Third-Party Services

ToneScope integrates a small number of third-party services strictly to operate core functionality:

  • Apple App Store / Google Play — process in-app purchases. Governed by Apple's and Google's respective privacy policies.
  • RevenueCat — validates and restores in-app purchases. See RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • Mobile advertising network (free version only) — serves banner ads. Ad networks may use device identifiers for ad targeting and frequency capping.

5. Children's Privacy

ToneScope is a general-audience music utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your recordings, settings, and app state are stored only on your device, you can delete this data at any time by deleting recordings within the app, clearing app storage in your device settings, or uninstalling the app. Purchase receipts held by the App Store / Google Play / RevenueCat are governed by those services' policies.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under applicable laws (such as GDPR or CCPA) to access, correct, or delete personal information held about you. Because ToneScope does not collect or store personal information on our servers, most of these rights are exercised directly through your device (for example, by deleting the app or resetting your advertising identifier).

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
